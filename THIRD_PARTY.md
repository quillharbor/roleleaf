RoleLeaf uses Electron for its desktop runtime, AndroidX WebKit for Android integration, and the Gradle Wrapper for Android builds. Build tooling includes electron-builder and @electron/asar. Dependency versions and package license metadata are recorded in the lockfiles and Gradle configuration.
